# Stomp, Tiptoe, Wiggle Zoo

Kids' movement song. Target runtime 2:15–2:30. Set as "Made for kids" in YouTube Studio.

**Hook (scored 72, STRONG, formula: The Question):**
"You have to find 3 animals hiding in this zoo before they run away. But which one is stomping?"

**Backup hook (70, WORKABLE):**
"You have 3 animals to copy, and every round gets faster. Can you freeze before you fall over?"

```
── HOOK (0:00–0:08) ──────────────────────────────
[ON SCREEN: A leafy bush shaking. Three pairs of eyes blink inside.
 The ground thumps. Camera wobbles with each thump.]
"You have to find 3 animals hiding in this zoo before they run away.
 But which one is stomping?"
[SFX: STOMP… STOMP… STOMP]

── THE TURN (0:08–0:18) ──────────────────────────
[ON SCREEN: The presenter character jumps up. Big bouncing text: "STAND UP!"
 A counter in the corner: 🐘 ? · 🐭 ? · 🐍 ?]
"When an animal comes out, you copy it!
 Every round gets faster. Ready? Stand up!"

── ROUND 1: SLOW (~90 bpm) ───────────────────────

[ON SCREEN: The elephant bursts out of the bush. Giant footprints land on
 every stomp. "STOMP" text shakes the screen. Counter: 🐘 ✓]
"Who's that stomping, big and grey?
 Elephant is here to play!
 Stomp, stomp, stomp your feet,
 Stomp, stomp to the beat!"

[ON SCREEN: A mouse peeks out of a tiny hole. Music gets quieter.
 Tiny footprints, a "shhh…" bubble. Counter: 🐭 ✓]
"Who's that squeaking, small and sweet?
 Mouse goes tiptoe, tiny feet!
 Tip, tip, tiptoe slow,
 Tip, tip, quiet… go!"

[ON SCREEN: A snake slithers across the bottom of the screen along a wavy
 rainbow line. The presenter wiggles hips side to side. Counter: 🐍 ✓]
"Who's that hissing, long and green?
 Wiggliest snake you've ever seen!
 Wiggle, wiggle, side to side,
 Wiggle, wiggle, wiggle wide!"

── CHORUS ────────────────────────────────────────
[ON SCREEN: All 3 animals in a row, each doing its move in turn.
 Icons light up on each word: 🐘 🐭 🐍 🎉]
"Stomp! Tiptoe! Wiggle! Zoo!
 The animals are moving, and so are YOU!"

── REHOOK (≈0:55) ────────────────────────────────
[ON SCREEN: The animals freeze, look at the camera, gasp. A speedometer needle
 swings up. Text: "FASTER!"]
"Uh-oh… they heard us! Now they're running FASTER!"

── ROUND 2: FAST (~120 bpm) ──────────────────────
[ON SCREEN: Quicker cuts, one animal per line, background scrolls faster.]
"Stomp, stomp, elephant feet!
 Tip, tip, mousey, sweet!
 Wiggle, wiggle, snakey, slide!
 Stomp! Tiptoe! Wiggle! Ride!"

── ROUND 3: SUPER FAST (~150 bpm) ────────────────
[ON SCREEN: The animals spin and blur, the speedometer hits red, confetti shakes.
 The presenter pretends to almost fall over.]
"Stomp-stomp! Tip-tip! Wiggle-wiggle-wiggle!
 Stomp-stomp! Tip-tip! Wiggle-wiggle-wig…"

── PAYOFF (≈1:50) ────────────────────────────────
[ON SCREEN: Everything snaps still mid-move. Music cuts to silence for 3 seconds.
 The animals hold silly frozen poses. Text: "FREEZE!"]
"Everybody… FREEZE!"
[3 seconds of silence]
[ON SCREEN: Counter fills: 🐘 ✓ 🐭 ✓ 🐍 ✓, gold star, cheering sound.]
"You found all 3 animals, and you froze! Did anybody wobble?"

── CLOSE (≈2:05) ─────────────────────────────────
[ON SCREEN: The animals wave. A big replay arrow pulses.]
"Want to go even FASTER? Press play and try again!"
```

## Production notes
- Keep the 3-second silence at the freeze; it is the payoff.
- The corner animal counter carries the goal for viewers watching on mute.
- One ask only: replay. No subscribe pitch.
